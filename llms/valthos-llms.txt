# Valthos

> Valthos is a New York City-based biodefense company developing frontier AI systems that identify biological threats and design medical countermeasures in real time. Backed by $30 million from the OpenAI Startup Fund, Lux Capital, and Founders Fund (October 2025), with a founding team from Palantir, DeepMind, the Broad Institute, and the Arc Institute. Valthos publishes no public API, developer portal, or open-source surface as of July 2026.
